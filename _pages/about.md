**CDSL (Control and Data Security Lab)** is a research lab in the Department of Electrical and Information Engineering at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech), led by Prof. Junsoo Kim.

Our main research area is **encrypted control (암호제어)** — designing control systems that can operate directly on encrypted signals, so that controllers, sensors, and actuators never need to see plaintext data. This combines control theory with modern cryptographic tools (e.g., homomorphic encryption) to protect cyber-physical systems from data leakage and manipulation.

### Education & Experience

- B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering / Mathematical Sciences, Seoul National University (2009–2014)
- M.S. / Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, Seoul National University (2014–2020)
- Postdoctoral Researcher, Automation and Systems Research Institute (ASRI), Seoul National University (2020–2021)
- Postdoctoral Researcher, Division of Decision and Control Systems, KTH Royal Institute of Technology (2021–2022)
- Assistant Professor, SeoulTech (2022–present)

### Selected Honors

- Outstanding Young Scientist Grant, National Research Foundation of Korea (2024–2029)
- Distinguished Dissertation Award, Seoul National University (2020)
